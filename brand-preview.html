Nursery and one-to-one sessions do different jobs.

Nursery gives children friendships, routines and the experience of learning in a group, things a one-to-one session can’t replace. A weekly session at home adds something different alongside it: an hour where all of an adult’s attention is on your child.

What school readiness means here

Being ready for school is about more than letters and numbers. It is also about confidence, communication and independence. Sessions build both, through play and at your child’s level.

Confidence

Feeling ready for something new

For children approaching or starting FS1 or FS2, including quieter children and those who find new situations hard.

  • Talking with a new adult and saying what they need
  • Listening and following instructions
  • Independence: managing their own things and having a go at new tasks
  • Naming feelings and settling into unfamiliar situations

Early learning

Strong foundations, at the right level

Playful practice in the skills that support early learning, matched to where your child is now.

  • Letter sounds, early blending and storytelling
  • Counting, comparing and number sense
  • Mark-making, pencil grip and scissor skills
  • Attention, listening and taking turns through games

Preparing for a school assessment? I can help your child feel more comfortable with an unfamiliar adult and new kinds of tasks. I don’t coach rehearsed answers, and no one can guarantee an admissions outcome.

Inside a session

Sessions follow your child’s interests, with real objects, open-ended materials and invitations to play, inspired by the Reggio Emilia approach. My job is to notice, listen and gently extend. A typical hour might look like this:

  1. 5 minHello and check-inHow are we feeling today? A song, a feelings card, a quiet start.
  2. 15 minAn invitation to playLoose parts, play dough or a story basket, set up around a current learning priority.
  3. 20 minFocused learningLetter sounds, number or fine-motor activities at the right level for your child.
  4. 15 minStory and talkA shared book, retelling and new words.
  5. afterBrief feedbackWhat we worked on, and an idea to try at home if you’d like one.

EYFS-informed, across all seven areas

The Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) is the framework used in British-curriculum nurseries and FS classes in Dubai. I plan with it in mind, alongside the non-statutory Development Matters guidance, so feedback connects with the language your child’s school uses. It describes typical development; it is not a checklist or a test.
